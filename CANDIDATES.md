# fridge-roast — kandidat & seleksi pairwise

Gate: 1 baris, maks 200 karakter, roast kulkas pintar.
Kriteria juri (detail JSON, bundle.competition.rules):
1. Roast kulkas pintar, joke diarahkan ke kulkasnya (app/firmware/ice maker).
2. Punchline mendarat: turn/kejutan di bacaan pertama.
3. Spesifik kulkas pintar, bukan gag gadget generik.
4. Wording ketat, tiap kata bekerja.
5. Sudut segar: hindari crack klise "ordering groceries" / "watching you".

## 12 kandidat (semua ≤200 karakter)
1. "My smart fridge updated its firmware at 3 a.m. and forgot how to be cold — now it's a $2,000 cabinet with Wi-Fi and a humidity problem."
2. "The ice maker only works when nobody wants ice; the second guests arrive it develops performance anxiety and serves lukewarm cubes of disappointment."
3. "My fridge's app sent me a push notification that the milk expires Tuesday. The milk expired in 2019. The app just found out."
4. "It has a touchscreen, a camera, and voice control, yet somehow still can't tell me why there's a single ketchup packet from 2017 living in the crisper drawer."
5. "My smart fridge needs a software update to make ice, a subscription to filter water, and a password to open the door — I bought a refrigerator, not a startup."
6. "The fridge keeps a photo log of everything inside, which means somewhere in the cloud is a high-res image of my shame cheese."
7. "Firmware update fixed 'ice dispenser issues' by disabling the ice dispenser. Patch notes called it a 'streamlined hydration experience.'"
8. "My fridge judges my groceries so hard the spinach wilts from embarrassment before the expiry date."
9. "It plays Spotify, shows recipes, and streams the news — the only thing it won't do reliably is keep chicken cold during a heatwave."
10. "The door alarm beeps if I stand there too long deciding, which is rich coming from an appliance that took four seconds to load its own home screen."
11. "My smart fridge's idea of 'energy saving mode' is turning off cooling at night and letting the ice cream file for divorce."
12. "It knows the milk is low, the eggs are old, and my diet is a lie — but ask it where the mustard is and suddenly it's offline."

## Seleksi pairwise (kriteria 1→5 berurutan)
- #4, #6, #8: gag kamera/menilai belanjaan — dekat ke klise "watching you" (kriteria 5) → gugur.
- #12: "ordering/knows groceries" klise → gugur kriteria 5.
- #9: daftar fitur generik, turn lemah → gugur kriteria 2/3.
- #2: lucu tapi panjang, punchline menyebar → kalah kriteria 4 lawan #1/#7.
- #3: twist bagus tapi setup 3 kalimat, kata boros → kalah kriteria 4.
- #10: turn "home screen" segar, tapi joke lebih ke layar daripada kulkasnya → kalah kriteria 3.
- #11: "file for divorce" cute, bukan turn keras → kalah kriteria 2.
- #5 vs #7 vs #1 (final 3):
  - #5: "not a startup" punchline kuat, spesifik (update/subscription/password), segar. Sedikit panjang.
  - #7: patch-notes angle sangat segar & spesifik, turn di "streamlined hydration experience". Dua kalimat.
  - #1: firmware-forgot-cold = turn paling cepat di bacaan pertama, spesifik, 1 kalimat ketat, kata terakhir "humidity problem" tajam.

PEMENANG SEMENTARA: #1. Perbaikan yang direncanakan setelah run pertama:
perketat wording (buang kata yang tidak bekerja) dan tajamkan kata terakhir
supaya turn mendarat lebih keras — edit asli, bukan kosmetik.
